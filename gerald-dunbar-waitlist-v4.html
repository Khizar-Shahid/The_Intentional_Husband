Meet Gerald & Sonya

A book written from a marriage, not a theory.

Gerald didn't write The Intentional Husband from a distance. He wrote it after a failed first marriage he takes full responsibility for, and 13 years into a second one he decided to get right.

Every month on the 22nd, the day they get married, Sonya finds roses and cards and sometimes an “I Love You” gift. Sometimes at the office, sometimes in the car. Even the day after an argument. Because choosing her wasn't something he did once at an altar. It's a decision he makes again every month.

This book is that decision, written down for every Husband and Husband-to-be who's ready to love on purpose.

What's inside

What this book will ask of you.

Twelve short chapters in two parts: before you get married, and how to love a Wife once you have her.

  1. Stop preparing for a wedding and start preparing for a covenant.
  2. Treat her like your Wife before she becomes your Wife.
  3. Heal before you marry, because unhealed men damage good women.
  4. Give her security, not swagger.
  5. Put the phone down. Your presence is the gift.
  6. Date your Wife long after you marry her.
  7. Cover her, don't compete with her. Her success is your success.

This isn't a book about being a perfect Husband. It's about being an intentional one.
